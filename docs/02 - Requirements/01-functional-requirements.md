# Purpose

This document defines all functional requirements for the Enterprise AI-Powered Real-Time EV Fleet Data Platform. These requirements describe the behavior and capabilities that the system must provide to satisfy business objectives.

---

# Scope

The functional requirements cover:

- User Authentication
- Vehicle Management
- Telemetry Data Collection
- Real-Time Data Processing
- Dashboard Management
- Alert Management
- Reporting
- User Management
- Audit Logging

---

# Functional Requirements

## FR-001 User Authentication

The system shall allow registered users to securely log in using their credentials.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-002 Vehicle Registration

The system shall allow administrators to register electric vehicles.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-003 Live Telemetry Collection

The platform shall continuously collect telemetry from connected EVs.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-004 Data Validation

The system shall validate all incoming telemetry before storing it.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-005 Dashboard

The system shall display live fleet information.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-006 Alert Management

The platform shall generate alerts for battery, temperature and connectivity issues.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-007 Reporting

Users shall generate operational reports.

Priority: Medium

Status: Approved

---

## FR-008 Role-Based Access

The system shall enforce role-based access control.

Priority: High

Status: Approved

---

## FR-009 Audit Logging

The platform shall record all critical operations.

Priority: High

Status: Approved
