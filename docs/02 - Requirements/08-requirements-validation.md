# Purpose

This document defines the Requirements Validation process for the Enterprise AI-Powered Real-Time EV Fleet Data Platform. The objective is to ensure that all requirements are complete, correct, consistent, feasible, testable, traceable, and approved before the design and development phases begin.

---

# Scope

This validation process applies to:

- Business Requirements
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria
- Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Change Requests

---

# Validation Objectives

The validation process ensures that:

- Business needs are correctly captured.
- Requirements are complete and unambiguous.
- Technical feasibility is confirmed.
- Requirements are testable.
- Stakeholders approve all requirements.
- No conflicting requirements exist.
- Every requirement is traceable.

# Validation Techniques

## Document Review

Business Analysts review all documented requirements for completeness and consistency.

---

## Stakeholder Review

Business stakeholders verify that requirements satisfy business objectives.

---

## Technical Review

Solution Architects and Technical Leads confirm technical feasibility.

---

## Walkthrough Sessions

Requirements are presented and discussed with stakeholders to clarify expectations.

---

## Prototype Validation

Where required, prototypes are demonstrated to validate business expectations before implementation.
