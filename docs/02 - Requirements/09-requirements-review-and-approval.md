# Purpose

This document defines the formal review and approval process for all project requirements. It ensures that requirements are reviewed by business and technical stakeholders, validated for completeness and correctness, and formally approved before the project proceeds to architecture, design, and development.

---

# Scope

This process applies to:

- Business Requirements
- Functional Requirements
- Non-Functional Requirements
- User Stories
- Acceptance Criteria
- Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)
- Change Requests

---

# Review Objectives

The review process ensures that:

- Requirements are complete.
- Requirements are correct.
- Requirements are technically feasible.
- Requirements are testable.
- Requirements align with business goals.
- Stakeholders approve implementation.

# Approval Rules

- Every requirement must be reviewed.
- Every review comment must be resolved.
- Product Owner approval is mandatory.
- Technical approval is mandatory.
- No development begins without approval.
