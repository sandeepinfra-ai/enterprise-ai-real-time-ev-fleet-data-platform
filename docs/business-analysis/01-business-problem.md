# Executive Summary

Electric vehicle (EV) fleet operators generate a continuous stream of telemetry data from thousands of vehicles. This data includes battery level, GPS location, speed, motor temperature, charging status, and other operational metrics.

The current system cannot efficiently process and analyze this information in real time, resulting in delayed decisions, increased maintenance costs, and reduced operational efficiency.

This project aims to build a centralized, AI-powered, real-time EV Fleet Data Platform to improve fleet monitoring, predictive maintenance, and business intelligence.

---

# Business Problem

The organization currently faces the following challenges:

- No centralized telemetry platform.
- Multiple disconnected data sources.
- Delayed fleet visibility.
- Reactive maintenance instead of predictive maintenance.
- High operational costs.
- Limited scalability.
- Manual reporting processes.
- Lack of AI-driven insights.

---

# Business Impact

These challenges result in:

- Increased vehicle downtime.
- Higher maintenance expenses.
- Delayed operational decisions.
- Reduced fleet utilization.
- Poor customer satisfaction.
- Limited business growth.

---

# Business Objectives

The proposed platform will:

- Centralize telemetry data.
- Process data in real time.
- Predict vehicle failures.
- Optimize charging schedules.
- Improve fleet utilization.
- Reduce operational costs.
- Support data-driven decision making.

---

# Success Criteria

The project will be successful when:

- Fleet monitoring is available in real time.
- Vehicle downtime is reduced.
- Maintenance costs decrease.
- Business users have live dashboards.
- AI-driven alerts improve operational efficiency.

---

# Conclusion

Developing the Enterprise AI-Powered Real-Time EV Fleet Data Platform will address the organization's current operational challenges while providing a scalable foundation for future growth, advanced analytics, and intelligent fleet management.
